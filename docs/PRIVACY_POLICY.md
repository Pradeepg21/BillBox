# BillBox Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

BillBox ("the app") is developed by Sabitri Gorai ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use BillBox on Android.

## Summary

BillBox works fully offline. Your bills, photos, products, warranties, reminders and budgets are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We do not run any server, we do not have an account system, and we cannot see your data.

## Information the app stores on your device

- Bill details you enter or that the app reads from a bill: store name, date, amount, GST, GSTIN, invoice number, payment method, category, tags and notes.
- Photos and PDFs of bills and warranty cards that you scan, pick or share into the app.
- Products, warranty periods, serial numbers, service history and service-centre phone numbers.
- Recurring bills, budgets and app settings (such as app lock).

This information never leaves your phone unless you choose to share or export it.

## Text recognition and document scanning

- Text on bills is read on your device using Google ML Kit text recognition. Bill images are not uploaded for this.
- The camera scanner is provided by Google Play services (ML Kit Document Scanner). Scanned images are returned directly to BillBox.
- Google ML Kit may send Google limited, non-identifying diagnostic and usage information (for example, API performance and errors) to keep these features working. See Google's ML Kit terms: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | To remind you about warranty expiry, due dates, return windows and budgets |
| Biometric / screen lock | Optional app lock, handled by Android; BillBox never sees your fingerprint or PIN |

BillBox does not request access to your contacts, location, SMS or full photo library. Photos and files are picked through the Android system pickers, which only give the app the items you choose.

## Sharing and export

BillBox only shares data when you tap Share or Export. You choose the app to send it to (for example WhatsApp, Gmail or Drive), and that app's own privacy policy then applies.

## Backups

BillBox data is not included in Android cloud backup. If you uninstall the app, clear its data or lose your phone, your bills cannot be recovered. Use **Export** to keep a copy.

## Deleting your data

Use **More › Settings › Delete all data** to erase everything, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, deletion is immediate and permanent.

## Children

BillBox is not directed at children under 13.
